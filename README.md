## The entry

> Our Monday standup is the only meeting where everyone stands, nobody stands for anything, and the printer still jams out of solidarity.

## Character count

135 characters — inside the 1–199 character gate (one single line, UTF-8 text).

## Why this line works

The joke is built from two details every office worker recognises instantly: the Monday standup, a meeting where everyone is physically on their feet yet nobody actually takes a stand on anything, and the office printer, which always seems to jam at the worst possible moment. The laugh lands in the last few words — the printer is not just broken, it has joined the protest out of solidarity, turning a piece of office equipment into the meeting's only participant with a spine.
